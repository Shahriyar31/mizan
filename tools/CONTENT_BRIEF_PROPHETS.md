## CITATION LOCK — unbreakable
- Qur'an is the backbone. Every layer should rest on named ayat. Quote Arabic inline in «»
  followed by the English meaning. Only quote ayat whose wording you are certain of; if unsure of
  the Arabic, give the reference and the English meaning only.
- Hadith: cite `Sahih al-Bukhari, ...` / `Sahih Muslim, ...`. Prefer book/chapter or narrator over
  a number. NEVER invent a hadith number or a grading.
- Isra'iliyyat (Jewish/Christian narrative material): do NOT present as fact. Where Ibn Kathir
  reports it with reservation, teach the reservation itself — this is a feature of the app.
  Model this on `idris.json`, which explicitly teaches Ibn Kathir's refusal to certify such reports.
- Where scholars differ (identity of Dhul-Kifl, whether al-Khidr was a prophet, the ordering of
  events), say plainly that they differ and name the positions.
- No invented dialogue, dates, place names, or numbers. Cut anything you cannot source.

## Tone
Reverent, vivid, teaching. Short sentences. Concrete imagery drawn from the Qur'anic text itself.
"the Prophet ﷺ" for Muhammad ﷺ; "peace be upon him" once per entry for others. No emojis.

## Schema (exact keys — cast directly by Dart; a missing key makes the file vanish from the
## app silently at runtime, so copy this list literally)
```json
{
  "id": "<given>",
  "type": "prophet",
  "sequence_number": <given>,
  "name_arabic": "<Arabic>",
  "name_english": "<given>",
  "name_translit": "<e.g. Yūsuf — with macrons>",
  "quranic_mention": "<e.g. Named 27 times in the Qur'an — state only what you can verify>",
  "group": "<given — the browse-section heading, shared with other entries>",
  "era": "<short evocative label unique to this prophet, e.g. The Patient One>",
  "teaser": "<one sentence, ~110 chars max>",
  "layers": [5 objects],
  "quiz": [10 objects]
}
```

### layers — exactly 5
| # | title | covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who He Was | lineage, people, place, what the world was like |
| 2 | The Call | the message he brought and how his people met it |
| 3 | The Trial | the hardest stretch — persecution, loss, waiting |
| 4 | The Sign | the miracle or decisive intervention, and its meaning |
| 5 | The Legacy | outcome, where he is honoured in the Qur'an, lesson for today |
(You may rename a title if the prophet's life demands it — e.g. Yusuf's "The Dream" — but keep
five layers and keep the arc.)

Layer object — all keys required except the two marked optional:
```json
{
  "layer_number": 1,
  "title": "Who He Was",
  "subtitle": "<6-9 word hook>",
  "content": "<1300-2100 characters of prose. No markdown or bullets. \n\n between paragraphs.>",
  "source": "Ibn Kathir, Stories of the Prophets",
  "quran_ref": "11:25",                                  // optional
  "hadith_ref": "Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Prophets"     // optional
}
```

### quiz — exactly 10, `number` 1-10 matching array order
Q1-Q5 FACTUAL: `"type": "factual"`, `prompt`, four `options` (`{"id":"a","text":"..."}`),
`correct_option_id` set, plus `citation` and `scholar_reflection`.
Q6-Q10 REFLECTIVE: `"type": "reflective"`, `prompt`, four `options`,
`"correct_option_id": null`, plus `slider_label`, `citation` and `scholar_reflection`
(both required here too). No option may be the obvious "pious" answer.
