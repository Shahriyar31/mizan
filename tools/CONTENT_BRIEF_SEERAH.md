## CITATION LOCK — unbreakable
Every claim must be traceable to a named source. You may state:
- Qur'an: exact surah:ayah. Arabic inline in «» guillemets followed by the English meaning.
  Only quote Arabic you are certain of; otherwise give the reference and English meaning only.
- Hadith: `Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of ...` or `Sahih Muslim, narrated by ...`. Prefer book or
  narrator over a number. NEVER invent a hadith number. NEVER invent a grading.
- Sirah: `al-Mubarakpuri, The Sealed Nectar` or `Ibn Sa'd, Tabaqat al-Kubra, Vol. III`.
No invented dialogue. No invented casualty figures, dates, or place names — the sirah sources
often disagree on numbers and dates, and where they do, say so in the text ("reports differ on
the number of...") rather than picking one silently. If you cannot source a claim, cut it.

## Tone
Narrative, cinematic, dignified. Present the events as events — place, weather, distance,
hunger, fear — not as a list of lessons. Draw the lesson only at the end of layer 5.
Short sentences. "the Prophet ﷺ". No emojis, no exclamation marks.
This section is the spine of the app: it should read like the best chapter of a book the reader
cannot put down, while every sentence remains defensible.

## Schema (exact keys — cast directly by Dart, a missing key means the chapter disappears)
```json
{
  "id": "<given>",
  "type": "seerah",
  "sequence_number": <given>,
  "title": "<chapter title, e.g. The Year of Sorrow>",
  "title_arabic": "<Arabic title>",
  "year": "<e.g. 5 AH — 627 CE, or 619 CE — the tenth year of prophethood>",
  "era": "<short evocative label unique to this chapter, e.g. The Siege That Never Broke>",
  "group": "<given — one of: Before Prophethood | The Makkan Years | The Madinan Years>",
  "teaser": "<one sentence, ~110 chars max, makes the reader open it>",
  "layers": [5 objects],
  "quiz": [10 objects]
}
```

### layers — exactly 5, this arc
| # | title | covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Setting | where, when, who, what had just happened |
| 2 | What Happened | the narrative core, told in order |
| 3 | The Turning Point | the moment the chapter hinges on |
| 4 | The Revelation | the ayat sent down about these events and what they settled |
| 5 | What It Left Behind | consequences, and the one thing a reader today should carry |
(Rename a title where the chapter demands it, but keep five layers and keep this arc. If no ayat
were revealed about the event, make layer 4 the closest Qur'anic principle or a hadith about it,
and say plainly that no revelation is recorded for the event itself.)

Layer object — all keys required except the two marked optional:
```json
{
  "layer_number": 1,
  "title": "The Setting",
  "subtitle": "<6-9 word hook>",
  "content": "<1300-2000 characters of prose. No markdown, no bullets. \n\n between paragraphs.>",
  "source": "al-Mubarakpuri, The Sealed Nectar",
  "quran_ref": "8:5",                                    // optional
  "hadith_ref": "Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Military Expeditions"  // optional
}
```

### quiz — exactly 10, `number` 1-10 matching array order
Q1-Q5 FACTUAL: `"type": "factual"`, `prompt`, four `options` (`{"id":"a","text":"..."}`),
`correct_option_id` set, plus `citation` and `scholar_reflection`.
Q6-Q10 REFLECTIVE: `"type": "reflective"`, `prompt`, four `options`,
`"correct_option_id": null`, plus `slider_label`, `citation` and `scholar_reflection` —
all three required. No option may be the obvious "pious" answer; give four honest positions.

## Quality bar
One file at a time, complete, then the next. No two chapters may share phrasing, sentence
rhythm, or opening move. Do not summarise the same event twice across chapters — each chapter
owns its material and may refer to a neighbouring chapter in one clause at most.
Report at the end: files written, both tool outputs, and anything cut for lack of a source.
